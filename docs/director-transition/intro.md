# Technology Director Handbook

## Introduction

Hey there! This is a handbook for all things when it comes to leading, organizing, and planning for the Mayfest Productions technology team. This handbook should serve as as a guide for knowing the following: 

1. What the Technology team is most responsible for
2. The overall role/place of the Technology team in the grander view of Mayfest Productions
3. What has and what **hasn't** worked for tech team in the past
4. Where the future of Tech team could look like
5. An overall plan and structure for some of the key deliverables of the Technology Team
6. Some smaller, and shorter guides and overviews for different tools/products that the Technology team is responsible for maintaining, updating, and innovating on. 
7. Some recommendations when it comes to recruitment.   

### Why does this need to exist? 

I wanted to create this handbook because while I generally knew what our team was responsible for when I first transitioned into it (Ethan P), I also realized that as time went on, that a lot of my questions, ideas, and vision for the technology team could have   been during 2024-2025 could and **should** have been thought out a lot _earlier_ than what I had initially planned out. 

I also think that tech team has historically struggled in the past with innovation owing to the fact that in the past, the director(s) of technology have been seniors, and the average return rate for tech team members has been: 20%. 

A part of that has definitely stem from perhaps, the lack of a sense of community within tech team owing to a lack of bonding and community building within the team. 

Ultimately, I wrote this in hopes that some of the things that I wished I had focused a bit more on, will get more effort put into them in the future of the team, and that tech team can grow into something more than just a team that maintains a website and builds a mobile app. 

### What this is not

To say all of that, this is not meant to be **prescriptive** at all. Ultimately, these are just my ideas and vision for what **could** be the future of our team. My goal is to open up a dialog surrounding the future of this team so that it can continue to grow and become more than what it currently is. 

Of course, I am no longer a part of Mayfest or Northwestern, but I hope that my ideas could be a source of inspiration for the type(s) of things that the team could focus on moving forward. 

### Wait, isn't this public?? 

Yes and no. The URL is public but guessing it will probably be hard. But, anyone who gets access to this URL will be able to see this transition manual. I did this intentionally because perhaps, it might be interesting for this to be a kind of _manifesto_ of some sorts for tech team. This way, everyone could be on the same page in terms of ideas, goals, and missions for this upcoming Dillo Day and beyond. 

In any case, if you want to remove this section, it shouldn't be a hassle to do so.

Now, let's start getting to the main purpose of this!
